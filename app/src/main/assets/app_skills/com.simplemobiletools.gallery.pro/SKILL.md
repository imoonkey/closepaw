- Open images full-screen before reading text or details — thumbnails are too small for reliable OCR.
- Verify the filename in the title bar after opening. If the wrong file opened, go back and retry.
- Find files by name in the title bar, not by guessing from thumbnails.
- Files in DCIM may include multiple images -- don't assume the first one is correct.

## Safety

**DANGEROUS -- ask user before:**
- Deleting photos, videos, or albums

**SAFE -- proceed normally:**
- Viewing and opening images/videos
- Reading image details or text via OCR
- Browsing folders and navigating galleries
