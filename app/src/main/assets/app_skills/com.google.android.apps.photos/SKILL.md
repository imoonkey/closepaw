- Open images full-screen before reading text or details — thumbnails are too small for reliable OCR.
- Pinch-to-zoom if text is still hard to read.
- Images are sorted by date. Scroll to find older images.

## Safety

**DANGEROUS -- ask user before:**
- Deleting photos or videos
- Removing items from albums
- Emptying trash

**SAFE -- proceed normally:**
- Viewing and opening photos/videos
- Reading image details or text via OCR
- Browsing albums and scrolling
