- Rename a recording from its 3-dot menu or by long-pressing it.
- Type the exact filename from the goal verbatim, including any extension characters.

## Safety

**DANGEROUS -- ask user before:**
- Deleting recordings

**SAFE -- proceed normally:**
- Recording new audio
- Renaming recordings
- Playing back recordings
