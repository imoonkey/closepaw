=== Device-level blocker evidence (OnePlus P0110, Android 14/15) ===

The accessibility service is silently NOT bound even though it is listed as
enabled in secure settings. This prevents real-device validation of flows
(d), (e), and the long-session portion of (h) because every session start
requires the AgentService accessibility binder to be live.
